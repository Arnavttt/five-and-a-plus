About This Course

AP® World History: Modern examines significant historical developments and processes from c. 1200 CE to the present, with a global comparative lens. Students study the connections between regions, the impact of trade networks, empire-building, revolution, industrialization, and globalization, developing skills in historical analysis, argumentation, and sourcing.

Key Skills Tested

  • Analyzing diverse primary sources from multiple world regions and perspectives
  • Drawing comparisons across regions, time periods, and themes
  • Writing historically-argued essays (DBQ, LEQ) with thesis, evidence, and complexity
  • Identifying long-term continuities and changes across civilizations

Scoring Tip: DBQ diversity point: make sure your documents represent multiple perspectives or regions. Outside evidence must be specific 'the Silk Road connected East Asia to Europe' earns a point; 'trade existed' does not. SAQs: answer in full sentences; vague, general statements earn no points.
