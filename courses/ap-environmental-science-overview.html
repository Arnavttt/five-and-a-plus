About This Course

AP® Environmental Science examines the natural systems of Earth and how human activities affect them. Topics include ecosystems, energy resources, land use, climate change, pollution, and sustainability. Students develop quantitative reasoning and laboratory skills to analyze environmental data and evaluate proposed solutions to environmental problems.

Key Skills Tested

  • Analyzing environmental data, graphs, and maps
  • Explaining environmental problems at a mechanistic level
  • Evaluating solutions and trade-offs for environmental challenges
  • Performing calculations (energy, population, carbon footprint)

Scoring Tip: FRQs are scored point-by-point one point per correct, specific statement. Explain mechanisms thoroughly ('because' statements earn points that 'yes/no' answers don't). For calculations, always show your work and include units.
