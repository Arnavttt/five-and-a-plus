About This Course

AP® Physics 1 (algebra-based) covers mechanics, wave motion, electric circuits, and introductory electrostatics. AP® Physics 2 (algebra-based) covers thermodynamics, fluids, electromagnetism, optics, quantum physics, and nuclear physics. Both courses emphasize conceptual understanding, scientific inquiry, and connecting physics principles to real-world phenomena.

Key Skills Tested

  • Applying Newton's laws, energy, and momentum to mechanical systems
  • Designing and analyzing experiments with appropriate variables and error analysis
  • Using algebra to solve quantitative physics problems
  • Explaining physical phenomena qualitatively and justifying answers with principles

Scoring Tip: Physics 1 FRQs often ask you to 'justify' or 'explain' write a sentence connecting a physics principle to the specific situation. Just stating a formula earns no credit without application. For experiments, always identify the independent variable, dependent variable, and controlled variables.
