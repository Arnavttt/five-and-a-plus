About This Course

AP® Psychology introduces students to the scientific study of human behavior and mental processes. The course covers biological bases of behavior, sensation and perception, learning, memory, cognition, development, motivation, emotion, personality, psychological disorders, and social psychology. Students develop research methodology skills and apply psychological concepts to everyday life.

Key Skills Tested

  • Defining psychological terms precisely and applying them to scenarios
  • Evaluating research designs and identifying experimental flaws
  • Applying psychological theories to predict and explain behavior
  • Connecting concepts across multiple units in integrated FRQ responses

Scoring Tip: FRQs are scored one point per correctly applied concept include both the definition AND the application. A definition without application earns 0; an application without proper terminology earns 0. Read each prompt carefully: 'analyze' requires both sides; 'describe' requires only the concept.
