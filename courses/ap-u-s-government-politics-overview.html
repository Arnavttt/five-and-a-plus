About This Course

AP® U.S. Government and Politics examines the design and function of the United States government and the role of citizens in the democratic process. Topics include the Constitution, civil liberties, political participation, institutions (Congress, President, Courts, Bureaucracy), and public policy. Students analyze 15 required Supreme Court cases and 9 required foundational documents.

Key Skills Tested

  • Analyzing the Constitution and its amendments in context
  • Comparing landmark Supreme Court cases and their reasoning
  • Interpreting quantitative data (polls, election maps, interest group data)
  • Constructing evidence-based arguments about government and democracy

Scoring Tip: The SCOTUS comparison FRQ always pairs a required case with a non-required case know the required 15 cases cold. Argument essay: take a clear side, cite two or three required documents/cases as evidence, address the counter-argument. MCQs often come with infographics read the axes and title before reading the questions.
