About This Course

AP® English Language and Composition develops students' ability to read and analyze complex nonfiction texts and to write in multiple rhetorical modes with clarity and precision. Students study argumentation, synthesis, and rhetorical analysis, learning to construct compelling evidence-based arguments and to recognize an author's choices and their effects.

Key Skills Tested

  • Identifying and analyzing an author's rhetorical choices (SOAPS/SPACE)
  • Synthesizing multiple sources into a coherent argument
  • Writing clear, well-organized, evidence-supported essays
  • Recognizing and employing effective rhetorical strategies

Scoring Tip: Essays are scored on a 0 to 6 rubric across three dimensions: thesis, evidence/commentary, sophistication. A defensible thesis that responds to the specific prompt earns the first point never write a broad generality. Commentary (explaining HOW evidence supports your thesis) is what separates 4s from 6s.
