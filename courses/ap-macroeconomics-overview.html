About This Course

AP® Macroeconomics examines the economy as a whole, covering GDP measurement, business cycles, unemployment, inflation, fiscal policy, monetary policy, and international trade. Students analyze economic models including the production possibilities curve, aggregate supply/demand, the money market, and the foreign exchange market.

Key Skills Tested

  • Drawing and interpreting macroeconomic graphs (AD/AS, PPC, money market, forex)
  • Tracing policy effects through multiple markets in sequence
  • Applying Keynesian and monetarist frameworks
  • Calculating economic indicators (real GDP, CPI, unemployment rate)

Scoring Tip: FRQ graphs must be fully labeled: axis names, curve labels, equilibrium points. Trace effects in the correct sequence (e.g., expansionary fiscal policy → AD shifts right → PL rises → real GDP rises). Short FRQs are often 2 to 3 connected graphs draw all of them.
