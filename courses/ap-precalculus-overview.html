About This Course

AP® Precalculus develops students' mathematical reasoning and fluency in polynomial, rational, exponential, logarithmic, trigonometric, and polar/parametric functions. The course prepares students for AP® Calculus by emphasizing function behavior, modeling with functions, and connecting multiple representations: graphical, numerical, algebraic, and verbal.

Key Skills Tested

  • Analyzing functions using multiple representations
  • Modeling real-world phenomena with appropriate function families
  • Applying transformations and inverse functions
  • Using trigonometric identities and the unit circle fluently

Scoring Tip: FRQs require justifications state the mathematical reasoning, not just the answer. For function behavior questions, identify domain, range, zeros, and end behavior explicitly. Calculator section: store intermediate values to maintain precision.
