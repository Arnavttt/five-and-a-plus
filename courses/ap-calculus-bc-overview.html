About This Course

AP® Calculus BC covers all topics in Calculus AB plus additional content including sequences and series, parametric/polar functions, and additional integration techniques. The course prepares students for college-level calculus and emphasizes conceptual understanding alongside procedural fluency.

  • Implementing algebraic/computational processes with and without a calculator
  • Connecting representations (graphical, numerical, analytical, verbal)
  • Building justifications and communicating mathematical reasoning
  • Applying calculus to model and solve real-world problems
