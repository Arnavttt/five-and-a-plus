About This Course

AP® U.S. History examines the development of the United States from pre-Columbian societies through the present. The course covers political, social, economic, and cultural history across nine chronological periods, developing students' historical thinking skills including argumentation, causation, comparison, and continuity and change over time.

Key Skills Tested

  • Analyzing primary sources for historical situation, audience, purpose, and point of view
  • Writing evidence-based historical arguments (DBQ, LEQ, SAQ)
  • Applying historical thinking skills: causation, comparison, continuity and change over time, contextualization
  • Tracing long-term developments across nine chronological periods

Scoring Tip: DBQ: use at least 6 of 7 documents, source at least 3, and include an outside piece of evidence. LEQ: earn the complexity point by explaining both causes AND effects, or by connecting to a different period. SAQs don't require a thesis write direct, specific answers with 1 to 2 pieces of evidence per part.
