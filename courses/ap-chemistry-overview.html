About This Course

AP® Chemistry explores atomic structure, chemical bonding, intermolecular forces, thermodynamics, kinetics, equilibrium, electrochemistry, and the properties of solutions. Students develop quantitative problem-solving skills and perform hands-on laboratory investigations to understand the physical and chemical behavior of matter.

Key Skills Tested

  • Balancing and interpreting chemical equations
  • Quantitative problem-solving (stoichiometry, equilibrium calculations)
  • Interpreting experimental data and laboratory observations
  • Applying models to predict chemical behavior

Scoring Tip: Show dimensional analysis clearly on every calculation partial credit is awarded per step. For lab questions, explain observations at the particle level. Don't forget significant figures: lose one point per final answer with incorrect sig figs.
