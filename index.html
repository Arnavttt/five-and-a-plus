Why students use Five & A+

Five & A+ puts everything you need to study in one place. Every lesson pairs structured notes with embedded videos from trusted channels like Heimler's History, Bozeman Science, and Professor Leonard, so you can read, watch, and check your understanding without ever leaving the page. More than 5,500 click-to-answer questions give instant feedback: pick a choice and the correct answer appears on the spot with a full explanation of why.

Key formulas are rendered in real math notation, FRQ strategy and common mistakes are flagged inside each lesson, and your scores, stars, and checkmarks save to your device automatically. Close the tab, come back tomorrow, and pick up exactly where you left off - no account, no paywall, no catch.
